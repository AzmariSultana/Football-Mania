Left stick aim · RT/A shoot · LB/RB curve · X power-up
  • Perfect Strike: release inside the green band on the power bar for a shot with no scatter that the keeper reads later.
  • Low power lobs over the wall but gives the keeper time; high power is hard to save, but above the red line accuracy drops.
  • Curve bends the ball around the wall and still lands on your marker. Wind pushes it while it's in the air.
  • Streaks multiply points (×1.5 from 3 in a row, ×2 from 5) and earn power-ups.
  • Every 5 goals a boss keeper steps in. Beat them for a bonus and a power-up.
  • Three misses and it's game over. Hitting the post won't save you.
